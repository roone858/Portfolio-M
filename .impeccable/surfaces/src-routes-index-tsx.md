# Portfolio home (/)

Mode: Experience. The work and the portrait lead from the first viewport.
Audience: recruiters and freelance clients, weighted equally. Their action: Download CV, email, or LinkedIn/GitHub. The proof is the four live project screenshots and the current role at ZonNet.
Constraint: all content comes from src/content/portfolio.ts. The portrait stays prominent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a film cutting bench flattened into one sheet. Mahmoud's shipped projects are frames strung on a perforated select rail, and his portrait is the frame that is selected and flagged. The page refuses the category's arrangement: a navy glow hero, a code-window graphic, and rows of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is ink black #0A0A0A with charred #1A1A1A and spool #2E2E2E panels. One ink, flag orange #FF5A1F, carries film grain and holds about a third of the screen across the rail band, the process strip, and the contact field. Running copy and screenshots sit in punched-white windows. Archivo is condensed to 62% for display and labels and used at normal width for body text. State is shown by marks: a sprocket-holed orange plate is the primary action, a hairline plate with holes is secondary, a folded tape flag marks "where you stopped", and a punched corner means unavailable.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor sees who Mahmoud is, that he ships real products, and how to reach him. They scrub the rail, open the work, and download the CV or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the left is a huge condensed "FRONTEND DEVELOPER" in white, with the orange line "BUILDING MODERN WEB EXPERIENCES." beneath it. Below that are a one-line positioning statement, the Download CV plate (primary) and See the work (secondary). On the right, the portrait sits as a tall frame in a vertical orange perforated strip, flagged as selected, with nothing over the face. Under it starts the full-bleed rail: a timecode ruler over an orange perforated strip of project frames.

FORM: Cutting Bench Select Rail (challenger operate-a-cutting-bench-select-rail; adopted by the user over the assigned Night Facade, which was candidate 5). Signature interaction: the rail scrubs horizontally and snaps to the frame pitch, and the last frame opened is persisted and re-flagged on return.
