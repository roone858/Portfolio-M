# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Hiring side:** tech recruiters and engineering leads in Egypt, Saudi Arabia / the Gulf, and remote-first companies. They skim fast, judge craft from the site itself, then download the CV or email.
- **Freelance clients:** business owners who need a site, store, or platform built. They look at real shipped work, then get in touch about a project.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Mahmoud Gamal. Success is a contact: a CV download, an email, or a LinkedIn/GitHub visit that leads to a conversation about a job or a project.

## Positioning

Frontend-focused full-stack developer: React, Angular, Next.js, and TypeScript on the front, with NestJS / Node.js and MongoDB / PostgreSQL behind it. He has shipped production sites in both English and Arabic (RTL): an educational platform, e-commerce stores, a marketplace, and an organization site with donation campaigns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with TanStack Start (React, SSR), Tailwind CSS v4, and lucide-react icons. The project is connected to Lovable.
- All content lives in `src/content/portfolio.ts`; components read from it.
- The primary CV is the frontend CV (`public/cv/mahmoud-gamal-cv.pdf`). A full-stack CV exists at `public/cv/mahmoud-gamal-fullstack-cv.pdf` but is not linked.
- The site is single-page, with sections About, Skills, Projects, Experience, Approach, and Contact.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the proof. Real shipped projects outrank claims about skill.
2. A contact is always one step away for both audiences.
3. The site itself demonstrates frontend craft: fast, accessible, and responsive.
4. Bilingual reach (English and Arabic RTL) is a real differentiator, so state it plainly.
